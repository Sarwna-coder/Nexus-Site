Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Data Collection

Nexus only collects data necessary for its core functions. This includes User IDs, Server IDs, Channel IDs, and Role IDs provided directly by Discord's API. We do not log message content unless it violates configured automod filters (which are immediately deleted after staff review).

2. Use of Information

Your information is used solely to provide bot functionalities such as:

  • Managing server economy profiles.
  • Processing moderation actions and logs.
  • Generating ticket transcripts for server staff.

3. Data Deletion

If you remove Nexus from your server, your server's configuration data will be automatically purged from our databases within 30 days. You can also request immediate data deletion in our Support Server.
